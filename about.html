About me

✨Hi, I’m Kaayla – Energy Healer, Spiritual Mentor, Medium, and Transformation Guide. I’m truly happy you’ve found your way here. ✨

A few years ago, I was a completely different person from the one you see today.

I lived what many would call a normal life: with a husband, two children, and a dog, in a house in beautiful Bucharest, Romania. My days were filled with responsibilities, tasks, and obligations. I was often challenged, sometimes overwhelmed - and there was little space left for myself.

Or rather, I didn’t allow myself that space. I simply functioned. It felt natural to take care of everything, to be strong, to hold everything together. Life was running the way it usually does.

Back then, I would never have imagined that only a short time later I would go through such a profound transformation. My entire life turned upside down.

My Awakening Journey

In July 2021, I made a discovery that would change my life forever:
I realized that I am clairaudient. Suddenly, I began hearing voices and connecting telepathically with souls - with people, animals, and even those who had passed on. Until that moment, I had never imagined that I possessed such abilities. I also wasn’t aware of my deep sensitivity and empathy - even though they had always been a part of me. This new perception opened a door to a world that had been completely unknown to me before.

I started observing myself more consciously, refining my perception, and truly listening to my inner voice. Step by step, I began changing my life and dared to step into the unknown.

Out of my old comfort zone - and into deep inner work with all my shadow aspects. Parts of myself that had long been hidden were now ready to be seen, acknowledged, and integrated.

During this process, I realized that we often carry energies within us that are not originally our own - patterns, imprints, and burdens that have been passed down through generations within our energetic system.

There can also be ancient soul contracts, vows, curses, or energetic ties that unconsciously prevent us from walking our true path and stepping into our full radiance.

These intense and sometimes painful experiences became a profound transformation for me - a kind of soul detox. I gradually released everything that no longer resonated with my true essence.

My body responded strongly to these changes, and I learned to listen to its signals while allowing my soul to guide me. Step by step, I embraced everything that wanted to reveal itself. I welcomed the process and became more and more receptive to the subtle energetic realms. This is how the door to energy work naturally opened for me. I began to perceive frequencies more clearly, consciously direct my own energy, and connect with subtle dimensions and higher states of consciousness - hearing, seeing, and sensing what is still hidden to many others.

Today, this is where I see my purpose:

To guide people in reconnecting with their own energy and light body, restoring balance between their masculine and feminine energies, and remembering their true essence.

Lightworker and Soul Healer

Perhaps you feel something similar within yourself.

I see myself as a Starseed with a strong connection to the Pleiades - a luminous field of consciousness associated with healing, awakening, and the remembrance of our soul essence.

Through my work, I bring light, inspiration, and high-frequency healing energies to support people in their transformation and reconnection with their soul.
