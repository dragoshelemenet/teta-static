What does it mean to live consciously with your energy?

It begins the moment you stop letting your mind lead the way and start trusting your inner compass again.

Your body speaks to you whenever you drift away from yourself. Maybe you feel a tightness in your chest,a lump in your throat,a knot in your belly,or shoulders that grow heavy and tense. These are not random aches. They are signposts,gently - or sometimes loudly - guiding you back to yourself.

Living consciously with your energy means you no longer overlook these signs. It means feeling your truth - and giving it space to be.

Sometimes it means simply pausing, breathing deeply, and asking yourself: Am I truly here - or caught up in the stories and energies of others?

This is where your return to yourself begins. You reconnect with your energy - and release what does not belong to you.
